01. Ownership & Transfer of Final Deliverables

Upon complete fulfillment of all applicable payment obligations for your project, ownership and full rights of use for the agreed final deliverables transfer to you, subject to the provisions of your project agreement and applicable intellectual property law.

This includes your customized page designs, custom compiled frontend code, unique graphic layouts, bespoke pitch deck presentations, and tailor-written editorial articles delivered specifically for your business. You hold the perpetual right to use, publish, host, modify, and distribute these finalized deliverables without recurring studio royalties.

02. Domain Ownership & Registrar Control

Client Domain Control: You provide, purchase, and retain 100% exclusive ownership of your domain name through your chosen domain registrar. Asnat Khan Studio never registers or holds client domain names under studio ownership.

Our studio assists in connecting your domain name to your hosted website by guiding DNS configuration or entering DNS records under your authorization. You retain total administrative authority to transfer, point, or redirect your domain at any time.

03. Customer-Provided Content, Logos & Trademarks

The client retains full and unencumbered ownership of all pre-existing trademarks, trade names, business logos, product photographs, proprietary copy, customer data, and brand materials supplied to the studio.

By providing these assets, you grant Asnat Khan Studio a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license strictly for the purpose of incorporating them into your project deliverables during active production.

04. Third-Party Themes, Plugins, Fonts & Licensed Assets

Modern digital websites frequently utilize industry-standard licensed assets to achieve superior performance and typography:

  • Web Fonts: Standard Google Fonts (such as Plus Jakarta Sans) are licensed under the SIL Open Font License (OFL) or Apache 2.0.
  • Icons & SVG Assets: Standard iconography is licensed under permissive open licenses (such as Feather/Lucide MIT licenses).
  • Stock Photography: Stock imagery or vector illustrations remain licensed under their respective stock library terms (e.g., Unsplash, Pexels, or commercial stock licenses).

These underlying licensed assets remain subject to their original third-party license agreements. Client ownership of the final website does not constitute exclusive proprietary ownership over third-party licensed building blocks.

05. Open-Source Software & Frameworks

Our studio builds robust solutions utilizing open-source libraries, web standards, and JavaScript packages (e.g. standard DOM utilities, normalization scripts, and framework runtimes).

Open-source software components are never represented as exclusively owned or created by our studio. They remain governed by their recognized open-source licenses (such as MIT, BSD, or Apache 2.0). The client receives the full rights and freedoms granted under those standard open-source licenses.

06. Reusable Tooling, Boilerplates & General Know-How

Asnat Khan Studio develops and maintains pre-existing developer boilerplates, common CSS architectural patterns, deployment automation scripts, and general programming know-how across projects.

These generic, non-client-specific utilities and foundational methods remain the intellectual property of the studio. In turn, the client receives a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to utilize these integrated technical elements as part of their delivered, deployed project.

07. Portfolio Display & Client Showcase

Showcasing completed live work is essential for an independent digital studio. Asnat Khan Studio may display screenshots, live URL links, and non-sensitive design excerpts in our studio portfolio, Fiverr professional showcase, and client review retrospectives.

Confidentiality & NDA Option: If your project involves unannounced products, proprietary business models, or sensitive internal data, you may request confidential handling or a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) in writing prior to delivery. Upon execution of an NDA, your project will not be featured in public portfolio showcases.

08. Client Warranty on Supplied Materials

The client warrants and represents that:

  • You own or have acquired all necessary commercial permissions, licenses, model releases, and property releases for any text copy, photography, graphics, trademarks, or media supplied to the studio.
  • The materials provided do not infringe upon the patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or privacy rights of any third party.
  • You agree to indemnify and hold harmless Asnat Khan Studio against third-party claims or damages resulting from the use of materials provided by you.
