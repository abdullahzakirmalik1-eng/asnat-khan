01. Overview & Studio Commitment

Welcome to Asnat Khan Studio (asnatkhan.com). As an independent digital studio specializing in complete website delivery, presentation pitch deck design, and editorial writing, client confidentiality and data integrity are fundamental to our relationship.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, store, protect, and manage the information you provide when browsing our website, requesting a project quotation, creating an account, or purchasing digital services. We are committed to transparency and handle personal information strictly in accordance with recognized privacy standards and applicable consumer data protection laws.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our studio platform, submit inquiry forms, or place an order:

  • Project Specifications & Inquiries: When you submit a request through our Get Quote form or project starter modal, we collect your full name, email address, company or business name, project timeline, budget parameters, and business overview.
  • Project Assets & Content: To build your website, format your slides, or write your articles, you may provide brand assets, logos, copy, photographs, target audience profiles, or domain preferences.
  • Order & Configuration Details: When you customize a package (e.g. Basic, Standard, E-Commerce, Pitch Deck, Writing), we record the selected options, extra products count, requested word counts, and project milestones.
Client Content Integrity: All business concepts, draft website copies, unreleased pitch decks, and internal operational data shared with our studio remain strictly private and are treated as confidential client materials.

03. Account Registration & Authentication

When you create a client account to track project progress or view active orders, we collect your full name and email address.

Our authentication infrastructure is managed securely through Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is never stored or visible to us in plaintext; it is processed and securely hashed by Google's audited identity infrastructure. When you sign in, security tokens verify your identity across authorized client dashboard sessions.

04. Payment Processing & Financial Security

All financial transactions for studio packages and services are processed using authorized, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Stripe or platform processors).

We never store, process, or retain full credit card numbers, CVV security codes, or bank account credentials on our web servers. Payment processors provide us only with non-sensitive transaction tokens, the payment status (e.g., paid, pending, failed), billing name, and order reference identifiers needed to generate receipts and confirm production kickoff.

05. Website Storage & Cookie Practices

We believe in honest, minimal technical footprints. Here is an exact explanation of how browser storage is utilized on our website:

  • Shopping Cart & Order State (LocalStorage & SessionStorage): We store your active package choices (e.g. asnat_cart_v1 and asnat_order_state) in your local browser so that your selections remain intact as you navigate between pages or review your order before checkout.
  • Authentication Tokens: If you sign into your client account, secure session tokens are held in client storage to maintain your login status across pages.
  • No Behavioral Advertising Trackers: We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers, behavioral data broker scripts, or hidden social media tracking pixels.

06. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely for legitimate business operations and direct service delivery, including:

  • Fulfilling Orders: Designing, engineering, optimizing, and deploying your website package, pitch deck, or written articles according to agreed specifications.
  • Direct Project Communication: Sending milestone updates, review links, clarification requests, and final project deliverables.
  • Customer Support: Answering inquiries submitted through the quote form or client dashboard message threads.
  • Platform Security: Monitoring against unauthorized login attempts, automated spam form submissions, and malicious exploits.
  • Accounting Records: Maintaining business invoices and transaction records as required by standard tax and commercial regulations.

07. Information Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing syndicates.

Information is shared strictly with trusted, essential service providers under contractual confidentiality safeguards for core functionality:

  • Google Firebase: User authentication and identity verification infrastructure.
  • Supabase / PostgreSQL Cloud Database: Secure encrypted cloud database storing order metadata, requirement records, and client communications.
  • Hosting Infrastructure (Vercel & Edge Networks): Serverless compute and static asset hosting to deliver website speed and global uptime.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information only if required by law, subpoena, or a lawful order from a competent legal authority.

08. Data Security & Retention

We employ robust organizational and technical defenses to protect client records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • All website data transmissions are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database access is protected by strict role-based access control (RBAC) and least-privilege administrative authentication.
  • Private administrative project notes are strictly isolated and never exposed in customer-facing API endpoints.

Retention: We retain client project records and order histories for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to fulfill ongoing support, warranty inquiries, and statutory accounting requirements.

09. Your Privacy Rights & Data Requests

Regardless of your geographical location, you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: You may request a copy of the personal information and order history we hold regarding your account.
  • Correction: You may request corrections to inaccurate personal details or project information.
  • Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten): You may request the deletion of your account credentials and project inquiry data, subject only to our requirement to maintain verified accounting and tax records where mandated by law.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@asnatkhan.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request". We review and process requests promptly.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and digital services are intended exclusively for commercial business owners, entrepreneurs, and individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors under the age of 16. If we become aware that personal information of a minor has been collected inadvertently, we take immediate steps to delete it.

11. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational enhancements, new service features, or adjustments in statutory regulations. When changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
