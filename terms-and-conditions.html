06. Free Hosting Provisions & Limitations

Where indicated in our packages, free hosting is provided using industry-standard static edge hosting platforms (such as Vercel, Supabase, or modern cloud CDN providers).

Free hosting includes standard monthly bandwidth and SSL security certificates suitable for typical business, portfolio, and marketing websites. Free hosting is subject to third-party infrastructure terms and fair usage thresholds. In the event that a client website experiences enterprise-level traffic exceeding standard complimentary tiers, the client will be notified and guided to appropriate high-capacity infrastructure.
