01. Nature of Custom Digital Services

Asnat Khan Studio provides custom digital design, website development, pitch deck presentation formatting, and editorial copywriting services. Because our work involves custom professional labor, creative conceptualization, and digital deliverables tailored specifically to your business, our policies differ from standard retail policies for physical mass-market products.

We do not treat all payments as automatically non-refundable, nor do we promise blanket full refunds regardless of work completed. Instead, we adhere to an equitable, milestone-based evaluation system designed to be fair to both the client and the studio.

02. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you place an order or pay a project invoice and subsequently request cancellation before project work or formal requirements confirmation has commenced:

  • You are eligible for a full 100% refund of the service fee paid.
  • Where the payment processor (e.g. merchant gateway) assesses non-recoverable direct transaction fees on the original payment, only the direct net merchant fee may be deducted where legally permissible.
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email before the formal project kickoff communication.

03. Cancellation After Project Work Has Started

Once requirements have been confirmed and active production has begun (such as site wireframing, custom frontend development, pitch deck slide structuring, or editorial research):

Refund eligibility is evaluated proportionally based on the milestones achieved and the creative work already performed. The client will be billed only for the verified work completed up to the date of the cancellation request, and any remaining balance for unworked phases will be promptly refunded.

04. Partially Completed Projects

If a project is cancelled while partially completed:

  • Retained Deliverables: Any draft design files, staging links, written copy, or assets created up to that point remain subject to the provisions of our Ownership Statement.
  • No Release of Unpaid Work: Unfinalized source code or live deployment on client domains is withheld until the applicable prorated milestone balance has been settled.
  • Transparent Audit: We provide a clear itemization of hours, milestone drafts, or page assets completed to support the prorated calculation.

05. Completed and Delivered Digital Services

Once a website has been approved, connected to the client's domain, deployed live, or final deliverable files (e.g. final pitch deck files or completed articles) have been handed over:

Delivery Milestone Rule: Delivered digital services represent fully rendered labor and irreversible intellectual property transfer. Once final project approval and delivery have occurred, payments are earned and non-refundable, except where required by mandatory consumer protection law.

If an issue arises after delivery, our studio provides post-launch assistance to resolve defects or verify that functionality matches the agreed package specifications.

06. How Refund Amounts Are Assessed

When evaluating a refund request for an active order, we use an objective milestone criteria model:

Project Milestone Stage Work Performed Refund Assessment Guideline
Stage 0 — Pre-Kickoff Order placed, requirements not yet confirmed 100% refund (less direct merchant processing fees if non-recoverable).
Stage 1 — Requirements Confirmed Brand analysis, architecture planned, assets reviewed Up to 80% refund (20% retained for onboarding & planning).
Stage 2 — Staging Draft Built Full site drafted, responsive design built, content placed Up to 40% refund (60% retained for design & core coding).
Stage 3 — Final Launch & Handover DNS connected, live deployment, deliverables approved Non-refundable (service completed and accepted).

07. Non-Refundable Third-Party Expenses

Certain legitimate out-of-pocket expenses incurred on your behalf at your express written instruction are non-refundable once purchased:

  • Third-party commercial font licenses purchased specifically for your brand.
  • Specialized stock photography or video asset licenses acquired for your project.
  • Client domain registrations purchased directly through external domain registrars.

Any such costs are disclosed and approved in writing by the client prior to purchase.

08. Customer Delays & Project Holds

If a project is delayed due to missing client content, delayed feedback, or unavailable DNS access, your payments and project are never forfeited.

Instead, after 30 consecutive days of client inactivity, the project status is placed on a temporary Administrative Hold. When you are ready to resume, you simply notify our studio, and work resumes from the saved milestone stage without arbitrary reactivation fees or penalties.

09. Duplicate Payments & Billing Discrepancies

If you experience an accidental duplicate transaction, system billing error, or incorrect charge, notify us immediately.

All verified duplicate payments and clerical billing errors are refunded 100% in full immediately upon verification. We encourage clients to contact our studio directly before initiating a formal bank dispute or chargeback, as direct studio resolution is faster and incurs zero processing friction for either party.

10. How to Submit a Request & Processing Steps

To submit a formal cancellation or refund inquiry:

  • Step 1 — Email Submission: Send an email to contact@asnatkhan.com with the subject line "Cancellation / Refund Request — [Your Order Number]".
  • Step 2 — Required Information: Include your full name, the email address associated with your order, the specific package purchased, and a brief description of the circumstances.
  • Step 3 — Studio Review: Asnat Khan will review the project milestone progress, communications log, and deliverables record directly.
  • Step 4 — Resolution & Disbursement: If approved, refunds are credited back to the original method of payment (via Stripe or payment processor) according to standard banking settlement schedules.

11. Statutory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy is intended to limit, exclude, or modify any statutory consumer guarantees, warranty protections, or cancellation rights that you may possess under applicable mandatory consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction that cannot be lawfully excluded.
